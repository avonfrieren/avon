disclaimer :
this may not be adapted to every map, i mostly play classic maps, and this method is fine tuned for **me** specifically, not an objective representation of what consistency training in order to deathless should look like
yes i know about other methods like the Blitzkrieg algorithm method from gd, just doing low death attempts, training checkpoint deathless and so on, and no i don't think this method should replace those but using it together instead
this method work for a map, but also a checkpoint, a campaign and so on, every segment in general
this method is not funny or entertaining, it's purely meant to be efficient, it can be frustrating, tiring or things like that

goals :
- prevent mind block in training
> by playing the rest of the map while practicing a room
- prevent stress in actual runs
> by having multiple high percentage segment deathless\*
- high consistency across all rooms
> by having cleared every room at least 3 times in a row or more
- more preparation, less attempts
> by having practice that much consistency\*\*

\*might as well get multiple high percentage of the map segments deathless, for example room 5/10 to 10/10 and even 1/10 to 5/10 to 10/10 in practice
\*\*doing that much consistency training will take a lot of time and energy, so less energy for doing **a lot** of attempts, but attempts should be really well prepared and successful, all that in order to clear in less attempts possible

tracking :
for a 10 rooms map example
**x5+**/**x20+**/**x3**/x10+/**x3**/x10+/**x3**/**x3**/**x2**/x10+
meaning first room has been done more than 5 times in a row, fourth room is not in bold meaning it's a transition/very low stake room

precepts :
- every room counts
> yes even that heart room where you only have to get the heart
- rooms are played in order
> do as you want, it's just simpler to track progress and doing the routine
- method needs to be done every session
> again as you want, i personally think it's a good warm up but it can be annoying if it's your 34 session or on a very long map

method :
play rooms in order, each one until you've cleared it at least 3 times in a row. every time you clear it, continue the run instead of resetting and keep going until you miss, because that's what practices the rest of the map. a hard stop may apply for very consistent rooms, but most of the time continue even if you've already cleared that room 20 times. 3 times in a row is arbitrary and may vary depending on the room's difficulty and place on the map : for later or harder rooms consider raising that number to 4, 5, even 6 or beyond.
